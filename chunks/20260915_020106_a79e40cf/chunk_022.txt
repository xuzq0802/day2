Of course, exemplary damages have a clear punitive aspect as well. As 
noted earlier, generally, punishment is regarded as a matter of criminal law 
and not private law. As a result, some scholars have argued that considerations 
of punishment should not be part of private law at all. 71 Ultimately, it seems 
that exemplary damages are here to stay; the main issues are whether they can 
be awarded for causes of action other than tort, and the extent to which they 
are available in tort itself, particularly in English law.
Chapter 9: Disgorgement of Gain
Chapter 9 considers accounts of profit and disgorgement. In equity, the account 
of profit is the remedy of choice where a defaulting fiduciary has made a profit 
as a result of their breach, and it is available subject to discretionary considera-
tions. By contrast, gain-based awards have not been openly available for com-
mon law wrongs and are generally available only if compensatory damages 
are inadequate and specific relief is unavailable. We consider the availability 
of allowances for work and skill, and the operation of discretionary factors and 
bars to relief.
