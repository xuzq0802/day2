CONCLUSION
In writing this book, we have tried to ensure that our discussion of private law 
remedies has a logical flow, and that recurring issues are dealt with clearly 
in each chapter. As in our previous writings on this topic, our intention is to 
create a coherent functional narrative of remedies which explains the law to a 
variety of audiences ranging from the newcomer to the specialist.
